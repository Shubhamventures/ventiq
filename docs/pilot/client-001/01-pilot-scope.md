# Client 001 Pilot Scope

## 1. Objective

Operate one agreed Client 001 fund on VENTIQ through a controlled, assisted pilot and prove that governed fund data, stakeholder workflows, approvals, investor access and operational evidence can be maintained in one system.

## 3. In-scope capabilities

The Client 001 pilot may use the following capabilities where relevant to the selected fund:

- organisation and governed fund setup;
- historical data intake and migration;
- investor master and commitment data;
- capital calls, receipts and distributions;
- fund financial/NAV history required for stakeholder views;
- investor SOAs and relevant historical investor documents;
- PDF ingestion for agreed document classes;
- Excel/PDF reconciliation and user-confirmed canonical resolution;
- portfolio data required for the selected strategy;
- compliance data required for the agreed pilot workflow;
- maker/checker migration review;
- five-layer activation across investor, PDF, portfolio, fund and compliance datasets;
- Finance and Compliance operating workspaces;
- Managing Partner / management views where supported;
- Investor Portal for specifically approved pilot investors;
- Investor Data Room and DDQ workflows for specifically approved content;
- governed document access and audit evidence;
- production monitoring and incident escalation.

## 4. Assisted-pilot boundaries

The following are allowed to remain assisted during Client 001:

- creation of the organisation and initial fund;
- source-file mapping;
- historical data normalization;
- exception resolution;
- first stakeholder invitation cycle;
- first investor release;
- first quarter-end rehearsal;
- support and incident triage.

Assisted means VENTIQ personnel may guide or perform controlled setup steps with the client's named owner. It does not mean bypassing access control, maker/checker, audit evidence or activation rules.

## 5. Out of scope for Client 001 unless separately approved

- broad self-service onboarding;
- automatic billing/subscription infrastructure;
- uncontrolled multi-fund rollout;
- bespoke integrations not required for the pilot;
- architecture changes solely for future scale;
- replacement of every existing client system on day one;
- immutable-ID refactors that are not required to preserve pilot security;
- broad custom reporting unrelated to the agreed pilot objectives;
- features requested after scope freeze without impact assessment.

## 6. Success definition

The pilot succeeds when:

1. the agreed historical/opening data is loaded and reconciled;
2. unresolved source conflicts are visible and explicitly resolved;
3. required datasets pass maker/checker governance;
4. the fund activates only after the agreed readiness conditions are met;
5. named stakeholders can perform their agreed operating tasks;
6. approved investors can see only their permitted fund/investor information;
7. documents and approvals are traceable;
8. at least one realistic operational cycle is completed;
9. production monitoring and incident escalation remain available;
10. Client 001 and VENTIQ sign the go-live acceptance record.

## 7. Scope freeze

Scope is frozen when the client sponsor and VENTIQ pilot owner agree this document and the data request list. Any later addition must be classified as:

- required pilot defect;
- approved pilot scope change; or
- post-pilot enhancement.
